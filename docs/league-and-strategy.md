# Strategy: build-first, not punt-first
Pick for the best player or fit in rounds 1–3. Your punts show up by rounds 4–5. Don't lock a punt before the draft.

| | **Build A: Efficient Wing / 3-and-D** (last season) | **Build B: Big-man + defensive stats** |
|---|---|---|
| Target | FG%, 3PM, FT%, STL, PTS, low TO | REB, BLK, FG%, PTS, lean STL |
| Punt | REB, AST, BLK | AST, soft FT% (3PM often) |
| Player types | Efficient low-usage shooters and wing defenders (Bane, Anunoby, Murphy) | True bigs plus two-way wings (Mobley, Kessler, Duren, Amen/Ausar Thompson) |
| Risk | Lower ceiling. Needs good mid/late role players | Depends on the right bigs falling to you |

**Trap:** don't slide into a *usage* guard build (Harden/Brunson/Young types). It looks like Build A but costs FG% and TO.

# Core principles
1. **Win 5–6 of 9 categories.** In H2H, winning a category by a lot counts the same as winning it by a little.
2. **Don't punt 3+ categories.** Too little margin, and one bad week sinks you.
3. **Corner blocks and steals** (scarce stats) with 2–3 real contributors. A 4th is wasted pick value. Assists are too common to corner.
4. **TO takes care of itself.** Usage stars come with high TO, and efficient wings avoid it. Don't draft for it.
5. **Draft timing:** rounds 1–3 elite guards/forwards → rounds 4–6 centers (the center run) → rounds 7–9 risk/upside picks → rounds 10+ sleepers and bounce-back players.
6. **Split rankings signal punt value.** Example: Giannis ranks #5 to #26 depending on the source. He's elite if you punt FT% and around a round-3 value if you don't.
7. Use the low-TO guards (Daniels, J. Murray, Murphy) as a **tiebreaker** in either build.
